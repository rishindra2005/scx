Algorithmic Autopsies: Four Identified Bottlenecks & Hardware Reality

Microarchitectural and algorithmic breakdown of why specific benchmarks diverged and how each was resolved.

1. HFT Spinloops (rlfifo vs Optima)

HFT uses tight lock-free ring buffers (__builtin_ia32_pause()). Naive FIFO pins them without moving. Optima demoted them to E-cores when runtime > 2ms. Fixed by enforcing same-CCX P-core affinity and preserving idle P-core headroom.

2. Schbench Wakeup Tail Latency

CFS used 0.75ms preemption slices to cut wakeup latency to 338μs at the cost of context switch overhead. Optima previously queued tasks behind 3–5ms batch slices. Fixed with immediate fast-burst quantum (1ms, vtime=now), cutting wakeup latency to 179μs P99!

3. iperf3 Socket Wakeup

Single-stream TCP loopback is softirq CPU-bound. CFS wake_affine keeps sender/receiver on the same physical 5.16 GHz core. Optima now checks SMT siblings and same-CCX idle cores on SCX_WAKE_SYNC, reaching 98.78 Gbps single-stream and 232 Gbps multi-stream.

4. Sysbench in Docker (12 CPUs)

12 threads previously got classified as batch, overcrowding the 8 E-cores in Docker while 4 P-cores sat underutilized. Solved via Capacity-Aware Dantzig Knapsack relaxation, keeping all 12 cores 100% saturated with compute tasks.
